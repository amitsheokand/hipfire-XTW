## Interpretation (non-claims)

1. **Speed:** Fuse MoE AR is the throughput leader on this GPU for greedy
   decode; Qwen dense 27B is VRAM-heavier and ~3.6× slower at the measured
   decode rate.
2. **Quality:** Qwen wins on chat genres; Fuse retains known model-level
   weaknesses (meta-chatter, attractors) documented in `T-hipfire-fuse-prod`.
3. **Not comparable across prompts without md5** — τ not reported (spec off).
