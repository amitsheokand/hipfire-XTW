## Why this run exists

DFlash 2 draft weights are F16. On gfx1201 they go through
`gemm_f16_wmma_mb8` → `gemm_f16_wmma_mb8_gfx12`, which register-blocks
**NB=8 (128 N-cols)** so vision-scale N can reuse the W A-tile. DFlash
decode is N=B=8: seven of those eight WMMA multiply zeros and `grid_n`
stays 1. `HIPFIRE_DRAFT_GEMM_DUMP=1` on the GPU-topk binary: K/V
M=1024×K=5120 at **44 GB/s** (weight is 10 MB; 631 GB/s roofline is
~17 µs, measured 243 µs). FFN gate/up 321 GB/s; FFN down 203 GB/s.

This row is NB=1 (`gemm_f16_wmma_mb1_gfx12`, 16-col N) when
`has_wmma_w32_gfx12 && n<=32`. N>32 keeps MB8. gfx11 unchanged.
